# Forensic Research Strategies

## 1. The Detective Framework (Travis/Hodgson)
* **Locard’s Exchange Principle:** "Every contact leaves a trace." Look for physical wear (sticky notes, hacks) to find unmet needs.
* **Safari vs. Zoo:**
    * *Safari:* Observation in context. For Discovery.
    * *Zoo:* Controlled usability testing. For Validation.

## 2. Tinbergen’s Four Questions
*Use to deepen shallow research requests.*
1. **Function:** Ultimate cause/survival value.
2. **Mechanism:** Proximate trigger.
3. **Ontogeny:** Developmental history (learning).
4. **Phylogeny:** Evolutionary history (organizational legacy).

## 3. Question Formulation (TEDW)
*Never ask Yes/No questions. Use commands to elicit stories:*
* **T**ell me about...
* **E**xplain how...
* **D**escribe...
* **W**alk me through...
