# Inclusive Design, Cognition & Mental Health

## 1. The Mismatch Framework (Kat Holmes)
Disability is not a personal health condition; it is a **mismatched interaction** between a person and their environment.
* **Cycle of Exclusion:** We design for our own biases -> We exclude others -> We validate with people like us -> Cycle repeats.
* **The Persona Spectrum:** Solve for one, extend to many.
    * *Permanent:* One arm.
    * *Temporary:* Arm in cast.
    * *Situational:* Holding a baby.
    * *Result:* Designing for the "One Arm" user improves the experience for the "New Parent."

## 2. Cognitive Exclusion (Microsoft Guidebook)
Design often fails users with diverse cognitive needs (ADHD, Dyslexia, Autism, Anxiety).
* **Motivation:** Does the design rely on high intrinsic motivation? (Support with extrinsic rewards/progress bars).
* **Memory:** Does it require working memory? (Support with "Recognition over Recall").
* **Attention:** Does it demand sustained focus? (Support with auto-save, distraction-free modes).

## 3. Mental Health Design Patterns
*Source: Microsoft Mental Health Guidebook*
* **Anxiety (The Need for Control):**
    * *Pattern:* "Direct." Use clear, binary choices. Avoid "dark patterns" that trap users.
    * *Feature:* Robust "Undo" / "Go Back" capabilities.
* **Depression (Low Energy/Motivation):**
    * *Pattern:* "Preserve." Don't let sessions expire quickly. Save draft states automatically.
    * *Feature:* "Soft" starts (easy entry points).
