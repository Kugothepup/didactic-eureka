# Worked example: ST-5, as the step might have run

ST-5 was set up before this step existed: Claude drafted the four research questions and the user
approved them. This is how the same study might have gone with the step, to show the shape. It's a
reconstruction, not a record of what was asked.

**Starting point (a topic):** "chatbots and conversational agents".

**Prompt 1, the decision.** Claude suggests: "Is this to inform designing a conversational agent, to
pick up where the market is, or something else?" The user's answer in ST-5 was general market
research. The trail would record that the study informs no single build, so findings are framed as
changes to try when designing an agent, not advice to the sites.

**Prompt 4, can proxy evidence answer it?** This is where the step would have paid off:

| RQ | Forum / postings | On-screen review | Only people can settle |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| RQ1 found, opened, introduced | thin (people rarely post about launchers) | yes, but 7 of 12 sites hid the agent | whether people notice it |
| RQ2 limits and data | forum would help; Reddit was out of reach | partly: first screens only | whether people understand the notes |
| RQ3 route to a person | forum is strong here | opening state only | whether people get through |
| RQ4 accessibility | a little forum evidence | yes: tab counts and axe | how assistive-technology users cope |

Seen up front, this says RQ2 needs a forum source early, and that RQ1 will mostly be a finding about
discoverability, not about introductions.

**Trail**
- v1: "How do chatbots introduce themselves?" Too broad, and describes a solution.
- v2: four questions (found, limits and data, route to a person, accessibility of the entry point).
- v3 (would have been added after the capture): add a question about whether the agent can be seen
  at all to a logged-out visitor, because only 3 of 12 sites showed a readable opening state.

**Mind map outline**
- Chatbots and conversational agents

  - RQ2 limits and data: forum, screens
  - RQ3 route to a person: forum, screens
  - RQ4 accessibility: screens, a little forum
