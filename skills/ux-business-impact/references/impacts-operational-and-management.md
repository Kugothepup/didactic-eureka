# Operational and Management Impacts

The two categories most available to an individual contributor and most often left unclaimed. Operational impacts are internal efficiency; Management impacts are team health and organisational function.

These matter disproportionately in two situations: a solo practitioner building the case for a second hire, and a performance review where the portfolio looks like twelve unrelated projects. Both are addressed in `translation-playbook.md`.

---

## Operational Impacts (O1–O9)

### O1. Increase Sustainability
Lower environmental cost of running and using the experience.

**Metrics.** Page weight and transfer size; server request volume per session; energy per transaction; print and physical material volume eliminated; device longevity supported by lower system requirements.

**Produced by.** S34 Design for Structure, S49 Design a Graphical User Interface, S95 Structure Content for Reuse, S12 Audit Content, S94 Migrate User Experiences.

**Caution.** Overlaps heavily with performance and cost work. Claim it where the organisation has a sustainability commitment; elsewhere it reads as an afterthought and dilutes the stronger cost claim.

---

### O2. Reduce Time to Design
Less elapsed time from brief to approved design.

**Metrics.** Cycle time brief-to-approval; review rounds per artefact; time spent in revision versus creation; time to first usable draft.

**Produced by.** S90 Create a Design System, S20 Create Experience Design Principles, S26 Write a Creative Brief, S83 Run Effective Design Critiques, S98 Establish Design Operations.

**Caution.** Faster design that produces more downstream rework is a net loss. Pair with F8 or a rework measure.

---

### O3. Reduce Redundant Effort
Two teams stop unknowingly building the same thing.

**Metrics.** Duplicate workstreams identified and merged; duplicate components or content items consolidated; cross-team artefact reuse; research questions answered from the repository instead of re-run.

**Produced by.** S24 Create a Site Map, S22 Create User Journeys, S21 Create a Service Design Blueprint, S89 Centralize Data and Insights, S98 Establish Design Operations, S23 Map User Flows.

**Caution.** This is the individual contributor's highest-leverage operational claim. It is what turns "I made twelve deliverables" into "I aligned twelve projects and unblocked parallel development." Requires you to name the specific duplication found and the team-hours it released.

---

### O4. Reduce Time to Develop
Engineering builds faster from the same design input.

**Metrics.** Clarification requests per story; rework after handoff; story cycle time; component build time; defect count at first QA submission.

**Produced by.** S43 Prepare Design Handoffs, S51 Annotate Designs, S29 Define Requirements, S30 Write Data-Informed User Stories, S90 Create a Design System.

**Caution.** Owned by engineering. Co-author or expect challenge.

---

### O5. Enable Transformation
The organisation successfully adopts a new platform, process or way of working.

**Metrics.** Migration completion rate and timeline; adoption of the new process by team; rollback and exception rate; training time to competence.

**Produced by.** S94 Migrate User Experiences, S82 Build a Human-Centered Design Culture, S05 Balance Research Parameters, S80 Understand Organizational Perspectives, S87 Establish a System for Governance.

**Caution.** Long horizon and heavily political. Claim participation and specific unblocking, not ownership of the transformation.

---

### O6. Improve Emergency Response
The organisation communicates and acts faster when something goes wrong.

**Metrics.** Time to publish critical updates; incident communication latency; accuracy of first-issued guidance; support contact spike duration after an incident.

**Produced by.** S57 Manage Publishing Channels, S61 Create How-To Content, S96 Adopt and Use a CMS, S99 Establish Content Operations, S46 Design Wayfinding Systems.

**Caution.** Only evidenced during an actual incident. Establish the baseline in peacetime — you will not get a second chance to measure the before.

---

### O7. Enable Nonspecialists to Create Design Artifacts
People outside the design team can produce acceptable work without a designer in the loop.

**Metrics.** Share of artefacts produced by non-designers; template and kit usage; quality-gate pass rate on non-designer output; design requests declined as self-serviceable.

**Produced by.** S90 Create a Design System, S92 Create a Style Guide, S71 Facilitate Creative Workshops, S98 Establish Design Operations, S82 Build a Human-Centered Design Culture.

**Caution.** Frame carefully. Poorly told, this sounds like designing your own team out of a job. The honest framing is that it moves designers off low-value requests and onto work only they can do — say that part explicitly.

---

### O8. Increase Productivity
More useful output per person, per unit of time.

**Metrics.** Throughput per person; work-in-progress and wait time; context-switching frequency; time-on-task for internal tools; hours reclaimed from workarounds.

**Produced by.** S63 Evaluate Design Solutions, S23 Map User Flows, S69 Know Your Toolset, S49 Design a Graphical User Interface, S65 Leverage Analytics.

**Caution.** For internal tools this is the direct monetisation route for U4 — seconds saved times frequency times loaded hourly rate. It is also the easiest place to overclaim, because reclaimed time is not automatically redeployed to anything valuable. Say "released capacity", not "saved money", unless someone actually reallocated it.

---

### O9. Reduce Time to Product Release
Ship sooner.

**Metrics.** Lead time from decision to release; release frequency; blocked-on-design duration; scope changes late in cycle.

**Produced by.** S29 Define Requirements, S35 Sketch Low-Fidelity Designs, S42 Assess Product Concepts, S43 Prepare Design Handoffs, S25 Create Product Roadmaps.

**Caution.** Shipping faster with more post-launch rework is not a win. Pair with R3 or a defect measure.

---

## Management Impacts (M1–M4)

### M1. Enable Organizational Clarity and Alignment
People agree on what they are building and why, and stop relitigating it.

**Metrics.** Decision latency on contested questions; consistency of stated goals across teams (ask three people, compare answers); meetings required to reach a decision; documented decisions with recorded rationale; reopened decisions per quarter.

**Produced by.** S19 Create an Experience Vision, S22 Create User Journeys, S17 Frame Opportunities, S16 Define Customer and User Types, S74 Align on Success, S01 Map Assumptions, S71 Facilitate Creative Workshops.

**Caution.** Often the true impact of an artefact whose stated purpose was something else. A journey map's real value is frequently that six teams finally saw the same picture — not that anyone used the map. Say the real thing.

---

### M2. Increase Employee Satisfaction
People are more able and more willing to do their work.

**Metrics.** eNPS; retention and regretted attrition; internal tool satisfaction scores; time lost to workarounds; role clarity in engagement surveys.

**Produced by.** S63 Evaluate Design Solutions on internal tools, S81 Manage a UX Team, S83 Run Effective Design Critiques, S66 Cultivate Relationships, S98 Establish Design Operations.

**Caution.** Internal tools are the most neglected high-impact surface in most organisations. A bad internal tool costs salaried hours every single day, which makes the O8 arithmetic unusually easy to run and unusually persuasive.

---

### M3. Create an Inclusive Work Environment
More people can participate fully in the work.

**Metrics.** Participation equity in sessions and critiques; accessibility conformance of internal tools; inclusion items in engagement surveys; accommodation request fulfilment time.

**Produced by.** S88 Embrace Accessibility, S71 Facilitate Creative Workshops, S82 Build a Human-Centered Design Culture, S83 Run Effective Design Critiques, S80 Understand Organizational Perspectives.

**Caution.** Measure conditions and participation, not sentiment alone. Sentiment measures are easily suppressed by the very dynamics you are trying to detect.

---

### M4. Create a Diverse Workforce
The organisation attracts and retains a broader range of people.

**Metrics.** Pipeline composition; funnel drop-off by stage and group; retention by group; accessibility of the application and interview process itself.

**Produced by.** S88 Embrace Accessibility, S81 Manage a UX Team, S07 Describe Research Participants, S82 Build a Human-Centered Design Culture.

**Caution.** UX influences the hiring *experience* and the accessibility of the process; it does not control hiring outcomes. Claim the surface you actually touched, and be careful with small-sample demographic data.
